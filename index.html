Hello INDIA!

India, officially the Republic of India or Bharat, is a vast country located in South Asia. It is known for its geographical, linguistic, and cultural diversity.

Geography & Borders

India is surrounded by the Himalayas in the north, the Arabian Sea to the southwest, and the Bay of Bengal to the southeast. India has mountains, plains, deserts, forests, rivers, and long coastal regions.

History

India has a very long history. It was home to the ancient Indus Valley Civilization and many kingdoms and empires. India became independent from British rule on August 15, 1947.

Culture

India has many languages, religions, foods, clothes, festivals, music, and traditions. Diwali and Holi are some of India's well-known festivals.

Economy

People in India work in farming, manufacturing, technology, healthcare, education, transportation, shops, businesses, and many other services.

Major Landforms

India has several major landforms including the Northern Mountains, Northern Plains, Thar Desert, Peninsular Plateau, Coastal Plains, and Islands.

Northern Mountains

The Himalayan range forms a natural high wall in the north. The Himalayas contain some of the tallest mountains in the world, and many peaks are covered with snow.

Northern Plains

The Northern Plains are fertile flatlands formed by rivers such as the Ganga, Yamuna, and Brahmaputra. The fertile soil makes this region very important for farming.

Thar Desert

The Thar Desert is a dry and sandy region located in northwestern India, mainly in Rajasthan. It has large areas of sand dunes and receives very little rainfall.

Peninsular Plateau

The Peninsular Plateau is an old highland region covering a large part of southern India. The Western Ghats and Eastern Ghats are important mountain ranges around this region.

Coastal Plains

India has long coastal plains along the Arabian Sea in the west and the Bay of Bengal in the east. These areas have beaches, fishing villages, ports, and fertile land.

Islands

India has two major groups of islands. The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are located in the Bay of Bengal, while Lakshadweep is located in the Arabian Sea.
