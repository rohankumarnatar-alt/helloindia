Hello India!

A simple page about India for kids.

About India

India is a very big country in South Asia. Many people live there, and there are many languages and cultures. It has tall mountains, wide rivers, deserts, and big cities.

Geography

India has many land types. In the north are very tall mountains called the Himalayas. There are also wide plains made by rivers and a hot desert in the northwest.

History

People have lived in India for a very long time. Long ago there were early cities and many kingdoms. India became independent from British rule on August 15, 1947.

Culture

India has many festivals, foods, clothes, and languages. People follow different religions and celebrate many special days with music, dancing, and food.

Economy

India has many kinds of jobs. People work in farming, making things, and in computers and services. Cities can be busy with shops and businesses.

Major Landforms

Northern Mountains

The Himalayan mountains are very tall and full of snow.

Northern Plains

The plains are flat and good for farming. Big rivers like the Ganga and Yamuna flow here.

Thar Desert

Hot and sandy, the Thar Desert is in the northwest of India.

Peninsular Plateau

A high, flat area in the south surrounded by the Western and Eastern Ghats (mountain chains).
